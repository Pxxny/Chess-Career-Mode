Venue city coordinates and population

Source: GeoNames populated places, via all-the-cities 3.1.0.
https://www.geonames.org/
https://www.npmjs.com/package/all-the-cities
GeoNames data: Creative Commons Attribution (CC BY).
https://www.geonames.org/about.html

This compact subset retains up to 150 populated places per country/federation.
Populations are the source dataset values, not live estimates. Coordinates are rounded to four decimal places. Missing cities are not assigned substitute coordinates.
Game map rendering uses bundled Natural Earth geometry and generated city scenery; see game-atlas-SOURCE.txt.
