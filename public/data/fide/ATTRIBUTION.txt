FIDE real-player snapshot for Chess Career Mode
Source: FIDE Standard Rating List, November 2026
https://ratings.fide.com/download_lists.phtml
Download: https://ratings.fide.com/download/standard_rating_list.zip
Source file date: 11 November 2026

20,000 selected active standard-rated players, with known birth year, from
222,878 eligible source records. This is NOT the entire FIDE database.
Selection: top 10,000 by standard rating, plus 10,000 sampled deterministically
across federations from the remaining players. See scripts/build-fide-snapshot.py
and sourceZipSha256 in players-2026-11.json for reproducibility.

Factual fields: FIDE ID, name, federation, sex, official title, standard rating,
birth year. Federation does not establish birthplace or citizenship. Given
name/family name ordering is converted from the source's comma-separated form.
Birth day/month and hometown are not provided; the UI shows year-only birth
data and unknown hometown. An internal January 1 placeholder is never displayed
as a factual birthday. Age is approximate. Peak Elo means the maximum in this
save, not a claimed historical career peak. All player styles, personality,
skills, potential and training habits are game estimates; game results and
future ratings are simulated. No real historical tournament results are invented.

This snapshot is used only when Real Player and start year 2026 are selected.
It is a November 2026 starting baseline, not January 2026 historical ratings.
Other start years use the separate curated historical real-player roster with
modeled rating curves. Normal database sizes retain fictional characters.

The game is not affiliated with or endorsed by FIDE.
